# Active technical workstreams — each ends in a reproducing artifact, not a narrative claim

1. **Full CBO acquisition** — Fetch the 20 pinned raw GitHub files. Retain exact bytes, Git blob SHA and SHA-256 receipt, CBO variable schema and all release dates. Test selected source extract vs all 3,334 baseline long-form rows and each accounting identity. `cbo_source.validate_full_source()` is implemented and awaits raw bytes.
2. **Treasury actual ledger** — Fetch all 2026 monthly MTS table 1-6c APIs and Daily Treasury Statements; reconcile cash fiscal year, trust fund transfers, tax refunds and debt-financing flows with CBO FY actuals. Do not equate obligation and outlay.
3. **Federal budget account graph** — Load full CBO `spending_detail` budget authority/outlays; classify agency, program, discretionary vs mandatory, trust-fund financing and interfund flows. Reject all duplicates and changes to coding after the release vintage.
4. **Revenue law and JCT** — Recreate 2026 effective statutes, date each threshold/deduction/exclusion; import official JCT individual bill/option score sheets. Run a versioned microsimulator to estimate tax liability changes only after model and data validation. No sum-of-single-option claims.
5. **SSA 1970–2100** — Fetch full official SSA single-year workbook; verify 11-year seed rates against workbook, then extract 2026–2100 intermediate, low and high assumptions for OASI/DI; build trust fund reserve-recursion with discounting, benefit cohorts and payability threshold tests. Current 11-year extract is verified, not full-run solvency.
6. **CMS 2026 Medicare HI/SMI** — Acquire 2026 expanded supplemental ZIP; extract HI taxable payroll, cost/income rates, trust fund operations, Part B/Part D, enrollment and per-beneficiary cost; validate 2025 past totals. Do not allow Part B savings to be called Part A trust-fund resources.
7. **Census ASEC survey engine** — Import the complete 2026 public-use CSV and replicate weights, inspect record layouts and actual field names, produce person/household/family universes, calibration against Census official 2025 income/poverty statistics and design-based standard errors. Never merge duplicate family and household records.
8. **Tax-incidence bridge** — Map person/household records to federal tax filing units, run Tax-Calculator or equivalent pinned version with two scenarios and legal policy file; then aggregate back to households; compare aggregate tax distributions with official IRS/JCT estimates.
9. **Population and distribution** — Generate actual weighted deciles and overlap categories for children, working adults, seniors, disability status, region and income; compare baseline modeled liabilities and transfers to CBO 2023 distribution and contemporary ACS/CPS. Census SDR sampling variance is now implemented for supplied full and all 160 replicate statistics, requiring real inputs and a provenance hash; official CPS production estimates are still pending source microdata.
10. **Medicare access/welfare constraints** — Medicare provider utilization, rural/urban, hospitalization and Part D data; tests for excess out-of-pocket burden, premium changes, provider access. The public dataset does not establish individual health outcomes.
11. **Macroeconomic and financing model** — Dated BEA/BLS/Fed/CBO forecast, debt maturities, term-premia scenarios and sensitivity of CBO financing residual. Static exact identities are established. Dynamic behavior is not a scored result until backed by credible macro model and tests.
12. **Legislative cross-agency package** — Single redline legislative text, phased effective dates and agency score requests, accompanied by frozen current-law date, input and output manifests. Combined CBO/JCT, SSA and CMS official results remain unavailable until those agencies release them.
13. **Independently reproducible sign-off** — Clean network fetch with immutable receipts; compare against official annual reports; document data revisions and release status. No scoring certificate without independent human and institution-level provenance audit.

The repository implements acquisition, full raw CBO validation gate, federal accounting, 2026 SSA annual arithmetic, household outcome table engine, sample splitting, and noncertification gates. Later technical workstreams are explicitly open.
