Medicare HI: the contingency finding

The draft phases in an additional 0.75 percentage points of combined employer–employee HI tax. In the frozen Trustees high-cost scenario, nonnegative year-end reserves through 2035 require an approximately 1.451–1.455 multiplier on that extra-rate schedule, considering published source rounding. That corresponds to 1.088–1.091 extra percentage points by 2030.

This does not establish within-year solvency, full reserve adequacy, a 75-year actuarial balance, behavioral responses, or official CMS scoring. It is a constrained algebraic stress test.

What's not priced yet

The record-level simulator in src/fiscal_data/v11_joint.py can compute source-specific mechanical interactions for covered wages, health benefits, income surtax, supplied itemization liabilities and VAT credits. There is still no verified complete household sample, no joint taxpayer/beneficiary links, no JCT tax-return microdata, no full CMS payment module, and no scored macro/interest effects. The 12-provision national reform remains UNSCORED.
